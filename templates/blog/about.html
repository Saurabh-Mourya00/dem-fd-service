About Me

Saurabh Mourya

I am a dedicated Django developer with a strong foundation in artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), currently completing my final year of BTech specializing in AI and ML. As I advance in my career, I am eager to further enhance my skills in AI and ML, focusing on innovative solutions that harness the full potential of machine learning within user-friendly web platforms.
